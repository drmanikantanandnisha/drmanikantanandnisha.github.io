Vaidya Dr. Nisha Manikantan

Director & Chief Consultant – Integrated Cancer Care, Sri Sri College of Ayurvedic Science & Research

Profile

Vaidya Dr. Nisha Manikantan, BAMS, MD (AM) is a globally recognized Ayurvedic physician, educator, and integrative medicine expert with over three decades of clinical, research, and teaching experience. She currently serves as Director and Chief Consultant for Integrated Cancer Care at the Sri Sri College of Ayurvedic Science and Research, Bengaluru, where she focuses on bridging Ayurveda with modern medical practice to enhance quality of life through holistic, patient-centered care:contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.

Leadership & Founding Roles

As a founding member of Sri Sri Ayurveda, Dr. Nisha played a pivotal role in establishing several key institutions within the Art of Living Foundation — including the Sri Sri Ayurveda Hospital, Panchakarma Treatment Center, Sri Sri Ayurveda Pharmacy, and the Sri Sri College of Ayurvedic Science and Research in Bengaluru:contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}.

Clinical & Training Expertise

Over her 32-year career, Dr. Nisha has conducted wellness consultations for more than 100,000 individuals across the globe and has trained hundreds of Ayurvedic and allopathic physicians in Nadi Pareeksha (Pulse Diagnosis), Marma Therapy, and Panchakarma. Her integrative approach combines Ayurveda with complementary modalities such as Meru Chikitsa, Craniosacral Therapy, Myofascial Therapy, Mudra Therapy, and Aroma and Color Therapy, creating a comprehensive model for preventive and therapeutic care:contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}.

Global Outreach & Recognition

An international faculty member and senior trainer with the Art of Living Foundation, Dr. Nisha has taught and conducted advanced Ayurveda, meditation, and breathwork programs in over 40 countries. Her lectures and workshops have been hosted at prestigious institutions such as the Krasnoyarsk Medical Academy (Russia), National Institute of Cancer (Mexico), Samaritano Hospital (Brazil), and the Universidad de los Andes (Chile):contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}.

Her contributions have been recognized with multiple honors, including:

  • 🏅 Vishalakshi Award for Excellence in Ayurveda (2009)
  • 🏅 Felicitation by Krasnoyarsk State Medical Academy (2005)
She has also served on the Expert Committee for Diabetes Lifestyle Protocols under the Madumeh Mukt Bharat Abhiyan of the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India:contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}.

Publications & Initiatives

Dr. Nisha is the author of Ayurveda Simplified – Body, Mind, Matrix, Herbs in the Garden, and Introduction to Ayurvedic Nutrition. She also founded several community and workplace wellness programs, including:

  • Ayurjagruti (Mind Body Matrix) – Ayurveda awareness and healthy living initiative
  • The Spark of Life – Workplace wellness and stress management program
  • Swasthya Yojana Shibir – Rural nutrition and preventive health initiative
  • She Matters – Women’s wellness and empowerment workshops
  • De-addiction and Trauma-Reduction Programs – Designed with medical specialists for mental health support

Current Work

Dr. Nisha currently serves as Chief Trainer at Sri Sri Wellbeing and Visiting Consultant to the USA and South America. She continues to lead global initiatives on integrative oncology, diabetes care, and Ayurveda-based preventive health, helping individuals and healthcare professionals rediscover balance through lifestyle, mindfulness, and traditional wisdom:contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}.

Philosophy
