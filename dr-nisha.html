Dr. Nisha Manikantan is an Ayurvedic physician, author, and Chief Consultant for Integrated Cancer and Diabetes Care at the Sri Sri College of Ayurvedic Science and Research. She co-founded the Sri Sri Ayurveda Hospital and has conducted extensive global outreach through Ayurveda and holistic lifestyle workshops.

Her expertise includes Nadi Pareeksha (Pulse Diagnosis), Marma Therapy, and Ayurvedic Dietetics. Dr. Nisha has written several books and continues to teach Ayurveda in an accessible, practical way for modern living.
