Dr. Manikantan

Ayurvedic Physician • Composer • Global Teacher

Dr. Manikantan (BAMS) is an Ayurvedic physician, composer, and global teacher whose life’s work unites music, breath, and ancient wisdom into one path of holistic well-being. For over 25 years, he has inspired audiences worldwide—bridging the depth of Ayurveda with the transformative power of sound to bring health, harmony, and joy to thousands.

Ayurveda & Healing

A pioneer of Sri Sri Ayurveda—the wellness wing of the Art of Living Foundation—Dr. Manikantan helped establish its first Ayurvedic hospital, Panchakarma centers, college, and herbal medicine manufacturing units. He has trained over 300 physicians and therapists globally and introduced thousands to Nadi Pareeksha (pulse diagnosis), personalized diet, and lifestyle practices rooted in Ayurveda.

His work also extends to allied sciences such as Meru Chikitsa (spinal alignment), Cranio-Sacral Therapy, Kalari Uzhichil (martial-art massage), and Acupressure—each approached through the lens of pranic flow and subtle energy. For more than two decades, his research has explored how sound frequencies, mantras, and rhythmic breathing influence physiological markers like heart rate variability, sleep, and stress.

Music & Global Impact

Simultaneously a gifted composer and vocalist, Dr. Manikantan has redefined devotional and meditative music within the Art of Living movement. His beloved compositions—Sundaranana, Hari Sundara, Krishna Govinda, Deva Manohara, and Madhava Mohana—are sung daily in satsangs around the world.

In 2016, he composed and directed a 130-singer ensemble for the World Culture Festival in New Delhi, performing before over 85,000 artists from 155 countries. His concerts blend live music, guided breath, and silence, creating experiences that dissolve boundaries of language, culture, and belief.

He continues to train the next generation of devotional musicians through the Art of Living Satsang Singer Orientation Program and the Art of Living Academy of Performing Arts (ALAP), mentoring artists to use sound consciously for emotional balance and social harmony.

Early Life & Journey

Raised in Kerala, India, Dr. Manikantan discovered his musical gift at the age of five. Trained in Carnatic music, he went on to lead the Kerala University Choir and perform across India. During his medical studies, he organized over 100 free medical camps—an early sign of his lifelong mission to integrate healing with service.

After completing his Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine, he recognized that true wellness lay in uniting sound, breath, and consciousness. Guided by Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, he began developing an integrated approach to Ayurveda and music for healing the body, calming the mind, and awakening inner joy.

Current Work

  • Research on sound, group meditation, and stress reduction
  • Development of music-assisted wellness protocols integrating breath and posture
  • Training facilitators in safe and effective sound sessions
  • Consulting for music and sound therapy clinics
  • Leading concert-meditation and corporate “Sound & Breath Reset” programs

Philosophy

“When music and breath flow together, the body heals, the mind quietens, and life becomes a celebration.”

To thousands worldwide, Dr. Manikantan is a bridge between science and silence—a physician who heals not just the body, but the rhythm of life itself.
