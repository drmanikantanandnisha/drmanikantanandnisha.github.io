Dr. Manikantan is an Ayurvedic physician, musician, and healer known for integrating sound, rhythm, and ancient medicine. A founding director of Sri Sri Ayurveda, he helped establish the Ayurvedic hospital, pharmacy, and college at the Art of Living International Center.

His bhajans such as Sundaranana and Hari Sundara are sung around the world. Through his teachings, Dr. Manikantan bridges science and spirituality — bringing peace and vitality through both medicine and music.
