Their Journey

Dr. (Vaidya) Nisha Manikantan and Dr. Manikantan have dedicated their lives to integrating the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda, the transformative power of music, and the depth of meditation. From the Art of Living International Center in Bengaluru, they have guided thousands toward holistic health and harmony.

Together, they have inspired countless doctors, students, and seekers across more than 30 countries — spreading the message that true wellness begins within.
